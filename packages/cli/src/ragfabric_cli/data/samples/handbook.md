# Northwind Employee Handbook

Northwind is a small company that makes weather sensors. This page is a short
introduction for new employees.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours you can work when
it suits you, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Where to find things

- Policies live in the shared policy folder, for example the leave policy.
- Team and reporting lines are listed on the team page.
- Questions about pay go to the finance desk.

## Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop and a headset on the first day. Report
lost or broken equipment to the office manager within two working days.

## Getting help

If you are unsure who to ask, post in the general channel. Someone on the
Platform Team will point you in the right direction.
