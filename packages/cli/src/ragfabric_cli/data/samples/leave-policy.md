# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full time employees of Northwind.

## Annual leave

Each employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues evenly across the year. Request leave at least one week ahead through your manager.

## Carry forward

At the end of the year, up to 10 days of unused annual leave carry forward into the next year. Any days above 10 expire on 31 December.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. A medical note is needed for absences longer than three days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Talk to your manager and the finance desk at least two months before your expected leave date.
