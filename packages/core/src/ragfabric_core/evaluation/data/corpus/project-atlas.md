# Project Atlas

Project Atlas is the migration of Brightwater's customer reporting warehouse to a new columnar store.

## Ownership

Project Atlas is owned by the Data Platform team. Aisha Bello is the project sponsor and Lucas Moreau is the technical lead.

## Budget and schedule

The approved budget for Project Atlas is 420,000 US dollars. The migration started in February 2026 and is due to finish in September 2026.

## Dependencies

Project Atlas depends on the IT Operations team provisioning the new storage cluster.

## Risks

The main risk is double running cost while both warehouses are live, estimated at 18,000 US dollars per month.
