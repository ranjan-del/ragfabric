# Travel and Expenses Policy

This policy applies to all business travel from 1 January 2026.

## Limits

The daily meal limit is 60 US dollars when travelling within the country and 90 US dollars when travelling abroad.
The hotel limit is 180 US dollars per night.

## Approval

Any trip longer than 5 days needs approval from the employee's director in addition to their direct manager.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later are paid only with approval from the Chief Financial Officer.
