# Brightwater Analytics Leave Policy 2025

This policy applies to all Brightwater Analytics employees from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next year with no upper limit.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 8 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Approval

Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Requests longer than 5 working days also need approval from the People Operations team.
