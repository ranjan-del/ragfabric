# Brightwater Analytics Leave Policy 2026

This policy replaces the 2025 leave policy and applies from 1 January 2026.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. From 2026, carry forward of unused annual leave is capped at 5 days; anything above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 10 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

New in 2026: parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first year after a birth or adoption.

## Approval

Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Requests longer than 5 working days also need approval from the People Operations team.
