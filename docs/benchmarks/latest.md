## How to read this

Refusals are answers that said the documents do not contain the answer; read them next to the hit rate, since a refusal with a hit is a generation failure, not a retrieval one. Metric definitions are in [docs/evaluation.md](../evaluation.md). Retrieval metrics are computed from the expected sources; generation metrics come from the judge named above; citation correctness is deterministic. A small question set means one question moves a category mean by a third; read the shape, not the decimals.
